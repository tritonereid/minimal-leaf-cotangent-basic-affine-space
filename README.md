## Abstract

Let $G$ be a simply connected simple complex algebraic group, and let $U$
be a maximal unipotent subgroup. Assume that $G$ is not of type $A_1$ or
$A_2$. We construct a $G$-equivariant closed Poisson embedding of the
minimal nilpotent orbit closure into $T^*(G/U)^{\mathrm{aff}}$. The image
of the minimal orbit is a symplectic leaf contained in the closure of every
positive-dimensional symplectic leaf. It is the unique positive-dimensional
leaf of smallest dimension.
